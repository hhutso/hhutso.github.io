Research [02]

Research Interests

I want to study how interactive systems can adapt to the different ways people think and process information, to make technological experiences more accessible and equitable. I’m especially interested in designing interfaces that meet people where they are, and in building algorithms that are fair, transparent, and unbiased, combining user-centered research with hands-on software engineering.

Research Experience

My research experience has prepared me to approach accessibility questions more rigorously. As an undergraduate researcher through the CRA UR2PhD program, I served as curriculum lead on a project that explored how interactive cybersecurity tools could be used to teach defense and deception. I designed three guided exercises, focusing on the students who would use them: what they needed to understand at each stage, how they would navigate the environment, and where additional guidance might be needed. I reviewed existing research and spoke with cybersecurity experts to understand how learners engage with cybersecurity training, then translated those insights into learning objectives and evaluation rubrics.

For more information on my experience in the CRA UR2PhD Program, here is an article I was featured in: NMSU computer science undergraduate research experience leads students toward Ph.D.
